# End-to-End ERP Management System for an IT Company

# Studio Lite LLP | IT Department – Q1 Project Assignment

# 1. Project Introduction and Objectives

# Project Overview

The End-to-End ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) Management System is a centralized web-based application designed to streamline and automate the daily operations of an IT company.

The system integrates essential business functions, including employee management, human resources, project management, task tracking, client relationship management, finance, and organizational reporting into a unified platform.

By implementing role-based access control and interconnected workflows, the application enables different departments to collaborate efficiently, manage resources, monitor activities, and make informed business decisions.

# Project Objectives

- Develop a centralized platform for managing multiple organizational departments and business operations.
- Automate routine administrative, HR, project, and financial processes.
- Implement secure role-based access control to manage user permissions and responsibilities.
- Improve project planning, task allocation, employee productivity, and workflow tracking.
- Streamline employee attendance, leave management, and performance monitoring.
- Manage client relationships, project budgets, invoices, and financial transactions.
- Provide real-time notifications, progress updates, and operational insights.
- Generate reports and analytics to support effective decision-making.

# 2. Project Features and Modules

The ERP system consists of eight major functional modules:

# 2.1 Admin & Organization Management
- Company profile and organization settings
- Department and team management
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- User permissions and audit logs

# 2.2 HR & Employee Management
- Employee onboarding and profile management
- Automatic employee ID generation
- Department and designation management
- Employee attendance and working-hour tracking
- Leave requests and approval management
- Employee performance tracking

# 2.3 Project Management
- Project creation and allocation
- Project manager and team assignment
- Project milestones and deadline management
- Project status and progress tracking
- Resource allocation and monitoring

# 2.4 Task & Workflow Management
- Task creation and employee assignment
- Task priority and status management
- Task deadlines and reminders
- Kanban board for task visualization
- Work logs and time tracking
- Task approval workflows

# 2.5 Client & CRM Management
- Client registration and profile management
- Lead management and tracking
- Client communication history
- Client project tracking
- Proposal and quotation management
- Client feedback management

# 2.6 Finance & Accounts
- Income and expense tracking
- Project-wise budget management
- Invoice generation and management
- Payment status tracking
- Employee salary records
- Financial reporting

# 2.7 Communication & Notifications
- Internal company announcements
- Employee notifications
- Email notifications
- Task and deadline reminders
- Approval-related notifications

# 2.8 Reports & Analytics
- Employee reports
- Project performance reports
- Attendance reports
- Financial reports
- Task completion analytics
- Centralized management dashboard
